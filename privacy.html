Privacy Policy: YourMoneyMathCoach Uploader

Last updated: 21 November 2026

YourMoneyMathCoach Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YourMoneyMathCoach TikTok account to upload their own educational videos to their own TikTok account as drafts.

What the app accesses. When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the basic account information TikTok provides for that sign-in (such as the account's open ID and display name). It uses this only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok inbox.

What the app stores. The access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, so uploads can continue without signing in again. Nothing is sent to any other server, and no data about any other TikTok user is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Removing access. The owner can revoke the app at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Manage app permissions) and delete the stored token file.

Contact. Questions about this policy: contact the YourMoneyMathCoach channel through its YouTube or TikTok profile.
